Membership commitment

Membership in the Association for Vibe Mathematics is public, and open to all people in the mathematical research community at the graduate level and higher. Membership is free and enables participation in the activities of the Association.

Members make the following general commitments as a part of joining the Association for Vibe Mathematics, with the understanding that they will make their collaborators aware of these commitments.

1. Members commit to respecting mathematicians’ freedom to use AI models, computer algebra systems, proof assistants, search tools, human collaboration, or any other tools in research, teaching, and mathematical communication, provided their use complies with academic and professional ethics. Members may also collaborate with commercial AI companies and tool builders; no affiliation alone is grounds for exclusion.

2. Members commit to taking responsibility for every mathematical claim they publish, including papers, reviews, referee reports, lecture notes, teaching materials, and books. AI-assisted or tool-assisted text is welcome when authors verify it, respect confidentiality, copyright, and applicable policies, give fair attribution, and disclose material assistance when relevant. Authorship belongs to accountable humans; using tools does not diminish it.
